buffer zones round built-up land, south (canton Esch)
=====================================================

what: how much of each commune lies within 200 m of residential / commercial / retail landuse,
or within 500 m of industrial landuse (nuisance zone, request from planning 03/2024).
result = area per commune in km2 + share of the commune:
  04_FINAL/buffer_area_by_commune.csv (for the report), .gpkg (for the map)

run order (qgis python, from the project folder):
  02_SCRIPTS/01_prepare_inputs.py
  02_SCRIPTS/02_buffer_landuse.py
  02_SCRIPTS/03_clip_dissolve.py
  02_SCRIPTS/04_area_per_commune.py
  python = /home/migrate/micromamba/envs/qgis  (micromamba run -n qgis python ...)

inputs 03_INPUT:
  osm_landuse_lux.gpkg            QuickOSM export, landuse residential/industrial/commercial/retail, whole LU
  geoBoundaries-LUX-ADM2.geojson  communes
study area = the 14 communes of canton Esch-sur-Alzette. ADM2 has no canton field, the names are typed
in 01 (check them if the boundaries are updated).

distances: industrial 500 m, everything else 200 m (agreed with M. on the phone, 03/2024).
  buffers done in LUREF (EPSG:2169) so the distances are metres. segments = 5 (qgis default),
  round caps/joins.
landuse polygons that only touch the study area are kept (select by location, intersect); their buffers
are cut at the commune boundaries in 03, so nothing outside the 14 communes is counted.
overlapping buffers are merged per commune (dissolve) so overlaps are not counted twice.
areas are planar in 2169, km2. the csv is rounded (3 decimals, share 1 decimal), the gpkg is not.

05_TEMP = intermediates, prefix = script that wrote it. keep them, needed to check steps.
